How it works

Open a standard personal account online — no branch visit required for most customers.

Step 1 — Prepare

Gather your ID and necessary documents. See the list below for accepted IDs.

Step 2 — Verify

Complete identity verification using our secure process (video or eID where supported).

Step 3 — Activate

Set up online access, choose your card options, and fund your account to get started.

Required documents

  • Valid government-issued ID (passport, national ID, or driver's license)
  • Proof of address (utility bill or official letter, recent)
  • Date of birth and national identification number if applicable

Additional documents may be requested depending on your country of residence or residency status.
